Bends

Rotate rolls the part about the arrow's direction first, then Bend angle curls it. Positive angles curl toward the palm. Radius is measured at the crease face of the foam (the palm face, or the Chone when curling toward the back); 0 is a sharp crease there, and the layers further out still wrap round it.

Editing

Like a pen tool. Click the outline to add a point. Between two points you can: drag the line to curve it, drag a point to move it (the line to the next point stretches to follow; on a long stretch only the 2" beside the point bends, the rest stays put), or drag a point's handles to change how the curve leaves it. Alt-drag a handle to break it into a corner. Select a point and press Delete to remove it. Drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom. The 3D view follows as you go; ⌘Z undoes.

Saving writes a copy of the DXF the item came from with only this piece's outline changed, exactly as shown (labels, notches and the other pieces stay). The item is then linked to the saved file.
